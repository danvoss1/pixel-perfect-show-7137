3D-Archivszene – Steuerung
View: drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Click a floor tile to select a room, or click an individual object.
Edit: turn on Edit layout, then select furniture. Move or rotate using the colored gizmo or inspector numbers. Add furniture or a wall segment from the left palette and click the floor to place it. Click an existing wall in edit mode to move, rotate, resize, duplicate or delete it.
Keys: G = move, R = rotate, Ctrl/Cmd+D = duplicate, Delete = remove, Esc = cancel placement or clear selection, / = search.
Im Browser speichern or download JSON to keep a portable copy. Desks, chairs, equipment and individual wall segments are independently editable. Room floor patches are retained from the original prototype; deleting a wall does not delete a room.
