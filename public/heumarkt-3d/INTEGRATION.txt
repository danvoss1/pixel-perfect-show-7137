HEUMARKT 3D · HEINZELS WINTERMÄRCHEN 2025
===========================================

Integration route:
  /3d -> iframe /heumarkt-3d/index.html

This revision replaces the former office geometry with an editable low-poly
reconstruction of the Heumarkt portion of Heinzels Wintermärchen.

Primary layout source:
  Official Marktplan 2025 supplied by the user / published by Heinzels Wintermärchen.

Additional visual references:
  - user-supplied photos of the rink, Stapelhaus/Zunfthaus, Schmied, market lanes,
    Christmas tree and entrance portal
  - official/published aerial imagery used to cross-check the ice-rink topology

Model coverage:
  - Futtergasse stalls and gates
  - large open-air ice rink and west/east loops
  - Reiterdenkmal in the west loop
  - bridge across the rink
  - Eisbär, Büble Alpe, Stapelhaus and Zunfthaus lodges
  - Kinderkarussell, Gondel, main Christmas tree
  - Goldgasse and Wintergasse stalls
  - Tor Unter Käster and Antikgasse transition with stalls 49–58 / Heinzelexpress
  - decorative light masts

Editor functionality retained:
  - orbit / pan / zoom
  - camera presets
  - search and object selection
  - edit mode with move / rotate / resize / duplicate / delete
  - add new market objects
  - save locally, JSON import/export
  - official market-plan overlay

IMPORTANT ACCURACY NOTE
-----------------------
The official market plan is an illustrated visitor plan, not a surveyed construction
or CAD drawing. Therefore booth sequence, relative placement, named landmarks and
rink topology are represented as closely as possible from the available sources,
while exact dimensions and metric coordinates remain approximate.
